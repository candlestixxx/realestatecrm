# CRM Segments & Bulk Workflows (User Guide)

Welcome to the updated Excel Legacy CRM. We have overhauled the way you manage leads to make them more actionable and AI-ready.

## 1. Understanding Segments (Workspaces)
In this CRM, **Workspaces** act as **Segments** or **Lists**. 
- Use the **Workspace Switcher** in the top header to toggle between different segments (e.g., "Web Leads", "Foreclosures", "Past Clients").
- Your entire dashboard—including Leads, Deals, and Tasks—will instantly filter to only show data within your active segment.

## 2. Bulk Lead Management & Sync Choices
The **Leads** page now supports powerful bulk actions and synchronization options:
- **📥 Sync from Queue:** Choose to pull fresh leads directly from the external MyPlus sync queue into your core CRM segment.
- **🔄 Sync from Workflow:** Pull leads generated during specialized intake workflows (like Foreclosures) into your main active list.
- **Bulk Action Bar:** Once leads are selected, a blue action bar appears. You can:
    - **Add to Segment:** Move leads between lists.
    - **Add to Deal:** Instantly launch a transaction pipeline from a lead.
    - **Add to Smart Plan:** Enroll leads in automated drip sequences.
    - **AI Assist:** Toggle on Gemini's autonomous communication for selected leads.
    - **Quick Comms:** Direct buttons for Call, Text, and Email triggers.

## 3. Lead Intelligence (Scrapers)
Inside any lead's profile, the **Intelligence** tab provides AI-powered research tools:
- **🌐 Social Scraper:** Use Gemini to locate and verify LinkedIn, Facebook, and X profiles for the lead.
- **🏛️ Public Records Scraper:** Gather property ownership, corporate filings, and voter registration data automatically.
- **Insight Panel:** Gemini provides real-time recommendations based on engagement scores and enrichment data.

## 4. The AI Assistant (Gemini)
Gemini 2.5 Flash is integrated as your default CRM assistant.
- **Status Panel:** Monitor AI readiness on the main Dashboard.
- **AI Assisted Flag:** Leads with active AI assistance are marked with a pulsing badge in the Leads table.
- **Drip Execution:** The AI analyzes behavioral data to personalize follow-up SMS and Email campaigns.

## 4. Workflow Performance Overview
The main Dashboard now features a **Split View**:
- **Overview:** Top-level metrics for your business.
- **Workflow Overview:** A dedicated section showing the 5 most recently active Workflows and Drip Campaigns across your workspace, giving you a real-time pulse on your operations.

---
*Tip: If you're new to these features, look for the **Onboarding Tour** pop-up in the bottom right corner of your dashboard.*
